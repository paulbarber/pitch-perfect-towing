Drive side moves your seat, and so the windscreen view, to that side of the car. Put the wheel on the side of your stronger hand. Car points up keeps the rig centred and turns the map around it, so left and right on screen always match the driver's left and right.

The goal

  • Reversing levels: get the whole caravan inside the amber pitch, lined up with it, then stop. The arrow shows which way the caravan's nose should face.
  • Forward levels: stop with the whole car and caravan inside the chequered box.
  • The pitch or box turns green when you're in the right place. Come to a stop and hold for a moment to finish.

Driving

  • Steering wheel: drag it round to steer, up to 1½ turns each way. Double-tap the wheel to straighten it, or press Centre.
  • D / R: choose forwards (Drive) or Reverse.
  • GAS: hold to move. Let go to brake.
  • ‹ and › at the top change level. Reset restarts the current level.

Views

  • Tap a mirror or the windscreen to enlarge it. Tap it again to go back to the top-down view. You can keep driving while it's enlarged.
  • On bigger levels the top-down view follows the car and caravan.
  • Tap the top-down view to hide or show the level description.
  • For a driver's-eye overhead view, set Overhead view to Car points up in Settings. A small compass shows north (red).

Auto demo

  • Tap Auto (bottom-left of the top-down view) to watch the computer drive the level. It restarts the level first.
  • It stops at each key point to explain what it is doing with the wheel. Tap Next to carry on.
  • The blue line shows where the caravan will go for the rest of the demo.
  • Touch the wheel, GAS or a gear button, or tap Take over, to drive on yourself from wherever the rig is. Runs finished after a demo aren't scored.

Guides

  • The amber dotted line shows where the back of the caravan will go when reversing, or where the front of the car will go when driving forwards, if you keep the wheel where it is.
  • Going forwards, the white dotted lines show where the caravan's rear corners will sweep. Watch them cut inside on bends.
  • Guides on / off switches these lines.

Hitch angle and bumps

  • The hitch meter shows the angle between car and caravan. It turns amber, then red, as you near a jackknife. Past 80° the rig stops until you pull forward to straighten up.
  • If you hit something, a red ring marks where, the part that hit flashes red, and a message names it.
  • Stars: ★★★ with no bumps, ★★ for one or two, ★ for more. Your best time per level is saved on this device.

Tips

  • When reversing, imagine holding the bottom of the wheel and move your hand the way you want the caravan to go.
  • Make small corrections early. Once the caravan starts to swing, it speeds up.
  • Going forwards, swing wide before a bend so the caravan doesn't clip the inside corner.
